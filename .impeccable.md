## Design Context
Users: The owner and visitors exploring a photorealistic DualSense reconstruction and watching it improve.
Use: Scroll from an assembled object through an exploded exterior teardown and intimate material studies. Freely inspect the object and visit a live record of independent visual criticism.
Personality: Sculptural, precise, dramatic.
Direction: Warm studio paper, enormous condensed typography, graphite, restrained electric blue. The object dominates. Camera movement explains construction. Actual iFixit exterior photographs are the visual standard. Reduced motion offers stable chapter views.
Principles: Real geometry over painted imitation. Honest review status. Legible editorial captions. Excellent mobile composition. Every visible control works.
